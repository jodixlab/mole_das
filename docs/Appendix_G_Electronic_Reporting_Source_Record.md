# Appendix G — EPA Electronic Reporting of Air Emissions Source Record

- **Official title:** _Electronic Reporting of Air Emissions_
- **Publisher:** U.S. EPA
- **Official URL:** <https://www.epa.gov/electronic-reporting-air-emissions>

## Structural relevance

Current EPA overview of ERT, CEDRI, and WebFIRE for stationary-source reporting.

## Pinpoint references used

- EPA states the system includes ERT, CEDRI, and WebFIRE.
- EPA states the ERT is used to create stationary source sampling test plans and reports.
- EPA states CEDRI accepts electronically submitted performance test reports for applicable programs.
